Join the work

Become a CoreXformer facilitator or supporter.

This path is for people who feel drawn to group development, experiential learning, honest reflection, and holding human spaces with care. Applying here does not create an instant login. It begins a review, onboarding, and readiness process.

  • Application first, login later

What this role needs

Presence, responsibility, and a reflective way of working with people.

CoreXformer facilitators are not only activity leaders. They help hold safe space, invite reflection, notice group patterns, and support meaningful learning after experience.

Who may fit well

  • People with facilitation, education, training, or group experience
  • Professionals who work well with reflection and human dynamics
  • Supporters who want to begin through shadowing and guided practice

How joining works

Move through application, onboarding, shadowing, and product-specific approval.

This system is designed to protect both the work and the people doing it. A facilitator should not enter live delivery only because they are interested. They should enter because they are ready for the product and the responsibility it carries.

Application

Tell us how you would like to enter.

Use this form whether you see yourself as a facilitator, a shadow facilitator, or a supporter who wants to begin close to the work. Admin reviews happen privately before any login credentials are created.

What to mention clearly

  • Your background with people, training, groups, education, or facilitation
  • The audiences you feel most called to work with
  • The products or themes that interest you most right now
  • Whether you want to begin through shadowing, support, or active facilitation

What happens after you apply

  • Your application enters the private admin review queue
  • If it feels aligned, you may be shortlisted or invited into onboarding
  • A real facilitator login is only created after admin review, not before
  • The goal is a thoughtful fit, not a rushed sign-up
