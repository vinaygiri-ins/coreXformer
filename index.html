Programs

Program 01 Understanding the Experience Helps individuals and teams pause, observe what actually happened, recognise assumptions and learn from their experiences.
Program 02 Planning and Strategy Develops the ability to think ahead, create a shared plan and act with greater clarity and purpose.
Program 03 Teamwork and Communication Strengthens listening, participation, coordination and shared understanding within teams.
Program 04 Emotions and Reactions Helps participants recognise emotions, understand their reactions and respond more consciously under pressure.
Program 05 Perseverance and Adaptability Builds the ability to stay committed, learn from failure and change direction when the existing approach is not working.
Program 06 Learning and the Way Forward Enables participants to convert experiences into practical learning, better decisions and meaningful future action.
Program 07 Core realization Roles, achievements and circumstances change with time, but they should not determine a person's entire worth. Respect begins when we learn to see the person beyond the position.
Program 08 The Power of I and We Helps participants discover their evolving strengths, identity and responsibility, and experience how self-aware individuals can create stronger collaboration, shared purpose and collective impact.
Program 09 Hidden Wings Habits that help individuals move beyond hesitation and perceived limitations.
Program 10 The Growth Mindset Helps participants recognise limiting beliefs and biases, remain open to feedback and view challenges, mistakes and new experiences as opportunities for continuous growth.
Program 11 Choosing the Greater Good Helps participants look beyond personal preference and emotional attachment, consider the impact on self, others and the wider system, and make responsible choices for the greater good, even when the right action feels difficult.
Program 12 Flexibility with Purpose Helps participants recognise what must remain firm and what needs to change, so they can adapt their thinking, emotions and actions while staying focused on the objective.
Program 13 The Efficiency Shift Helps participants recognise when growth requires new resources and when progress depends on using existing resources more intelligently, shifting from constant expansion to sustainable efficiency.
Program 14 From Chaos to Coherence Helps participants accept the uncertainty and disorder that often accompany new beginnings, remain engaged through the process, and gradually create clarity, alignment and workable solutions.
Program 15 Progress Before Perfection Helps participants move beyond perfectionism, begin before everything feels ready, and use early imperfections as opportunities to learn, improve and build something meaningful.
Program 16 Make Life Light Helps participants recognise how unnecessary seriousness and complexity create emotional weight, and practise simpler, lighter ways of thinking, responding and moving forward.
Program 17 Identities in Motion Helps participants understand how identities are shaped by experience, relationships and aspirations, and learn to accept changes in themselves and others with empathy and openness.
Program 18 Understanding the Seeking Mind Helps participants notice how comparison and social conditioning can create a sense of "not enough," leading the mind to continually seek fulfilment, and explores how awareness can reduce the burden of this endless pursuit.

Founder and Facilitation

A naval officer's discipline, a facilitator's warmth, and a steady eye for human growth.

CoreXformer is led by Lt. Commander Vinay Giri (Retd.), an experiential learning consultant, leadership facilitator, and ex-Naval officer. His work brings together over 15 years of leadership experience across the Indian Navy and the corporate sector with a deep interest in inner transformation and human growth.

Indian Navy JPMorgan Chase & Co. Pegasus Academy Experiential learning

The work behind the room

CoreXformer creates a pause where people can notice how they show up.

A former software developer at JPMorgan Chase & Co. and an associate with Pegasus Academy, Vinay now creates experiential spaces that help individuals and teams rediscover themselves through play, reflection, and meaningful connection.

He has led teams in the Navy, built technology at JPMorgan, and held space for people in both villages and boardrooms. Across those spaces, one truth stands out: people often move fast without pausing to feel, reflect, and notice the unconscious patterns shaping how they show up. CoreXformer exists to create that pause.

01 Service

Leadership under pressure, responsibility, and human decision-making in real situations.

02 Systems

Technology and corporate work that sharpened clarity, structure, and disciplined execution.

03 Reflection

Facilitation across villages, classrooms, and boardrooms where people can slow down and learn from experience.

How the room is held

Clear, warm, and context-aware.

01 Warm facilitation

Participants are welcomed into a space that values honesty, emotional safety, and respectful reflection.

02 Context-first design

The session is shaped around your audience, objective, venue reality, and the energy of the group you bring.

03 Safe reflection

Differences, mistakes, and mixed emotions are held carefully so learning can emerge without shame or judgment.

04 Clear next steps

Inquiry, planning, and delivery are treated as one continuous journey rather than a disconnected booking transaction.

The work is not to impress a group with an activity. The work is to create a pause where people can see themselves more honestly.
