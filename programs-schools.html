Stage fit | Joining a New Group · Building Belonging

Belong & Connect

Open full program page

Help students feel safe, seen, welcomed, and emotionally connected in a group space.

When to choose this

  • New academic year or new class section
  • New club, house team, or student group
  • Low comfort and low participation
  • A group that feels socially distant

What students explore

  • Comfort and hesitation
  • Trust in first interactions
  • Openness and peer familiarity
  • What helps people feel included

Likely outcomes

  • Stronger belonging and early trust
  • Healthier peer familiarity
  • More willingness to participate
  • Improved sense of group safety

Stage fit | Working Together

Team Up & Solve

Request this program

Help students work together more effectively toward shared goals.

When to choose this

  • Student group work is weak or uneven
  • Students do not listen to one another
  • Participation is imbalanced
  • Coordination is poor

What students explore

  • Listening and role sharing
  • Coordination and contribution
  • Supporting others while staying on task

Likely outcomes

  • Better teamwork and cooperation
  • Stronger listening
  • More shared responsibility
  • More effective group participation

Stage fit | Handling Pressure

Feel, Pause, Respond

Request this program

Help students become more aware of emotions and reactions when situations get difficult.

When to choose this

  • Students get frustrated quickly
  • Pressure changes behavior sharply
  • Students give up early
  • Mistakes create escalation

What students explore

  • Frustration and fear of failure
  • Excitement, impulsiveness, and impatience
  • Pressure response and recovery after mistakes

Likely outcomes

  • Stronger emotional awareness
  • Improved self-regulation
  • Better response to mistakes
  • More resilience under challenge

Stage fit | Navigating Difference

Respect in Difference

Request this program

Help students understand difference with greater empathy and less reactive judgment.

When to choose this

  • Cliques are visible
  • Peer conflict repeats
  • Exclusion or teasing is affecting the group
  • Students struggle to cooperate across differences

What students explore

  • Perspective differences and assumptions
  • Peer reactions and inclusion patterns
  • Impact of judgment on group dynamics

Likely outcomes

  • More empathy and acceptance of difference
  • Healthier peer communication
  • Stronger inclusion
  • Better team functioning across varied personalities

Stage fit | Belonging · Participation

Courage & Confidence in Action

Request this program

Help students move beyond hesitation and participate more fully with confidence and initiative.

When to choose this

  • Students hesitate to speak up
  • Students doubt their contribution
  • Fear of embarrassment or failure is high
  • Participation is low even when potential is high

What students explore

  • Self-doubt and courage
  • Voice and initiative
  • What blocks contribution

Likely outcomes

  • Stronger confidence
  • Greater willingness to try
  • More voice and better participation
  • Healthier relationship with mistakes

Stage fit | Growing Leadership

Student Leadership in Action

Request this program

Help student leaders understand leadership as responsibility, influence, awareness, and support for the whole group.

When to choose this

  • Student leaders need development
  • Responsibility exists but maturity is uneven
  • Leadership roles are formal but not yet deeply understood
  • The school wants leaders who can support peers, not only direct them

What students explore

  • Initiative and responsibility
  • Influence and delegation
  • Support and leadership under pressure

Likely outcomes

  • Stronger leadership awareness
  • Healthier influence and peer support
  • Better stewardship of group culture

What students may leave with

Belonging, teamwork, emotional awareness, confidence, and stronger responsibility.

The exact outcome depends on the program chosen, but the school line as a whole is designed to help students feel more connected, understand themselves and peers better, and move with more awareness inside shared objectives.

  • Stronger belonging and peer connection
  • Healthier teamwork and cooperation
  • More emotional awareness under challenge
  • Greater respect across difference
  • More confidence in participation
  • Deeper student leadership and responsibility

Need a different fit?

If your student reality does not fit one program neatly, we can still shape the right school journey.

The six programs on this page are meant to reduce confusion, not force every school into a rigid box. If your students are carrying a blended reality, we can start with the closest fit and then adapt the journey to your context, age group, and current challenge.

Helpful starting details

  • Student age group or class range
  • Current challenge in the group
  • Desired outcome or shift
  • Approximate number of participants
