Helps participants recognise limiting beliefs and biases, remain open to feedback and view challenges, mistakes and new experiences as opportunities for continuous growth.

Program 11 Beyond the Script

Helps participants identify deeply structured assumptions and outdated patterns, enabling them to change decisions and strategies according to changing situations rather than remaining trapped in fixed ways of thinking.
