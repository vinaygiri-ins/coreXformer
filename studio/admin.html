Private handoff rules

What should happen after a review decision

  • Submitted or screening The person stays in admin review and should not receive a private login yet.
  • Invited to onboarding Admin sends the onboarding note and prepares candidate-stage access through the shared studio entry.
  • Candidate stage The facilitator should see `My Journey` first: profile, foundations, product path, and related issues.
  • Approved later `My Work` should only open after product-specific readiness and real operational approval become clear.
